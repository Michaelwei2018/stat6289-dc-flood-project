- Units: mm (daily total). Missing values: NaN (no observation for that date); 0.0 means measured zero rainfall.
- Day boundary: First-order/airport stations (USW): midnight-to-midnight local standard time. COOP (USC) and CoCoRaHS (US1): 24 h ending at the observation time (obs_time, often ~07:00 local) on the stated date, so most of the rain belongs to the previous calendar day.
